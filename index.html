Honours Dissertation

An Integrated Geospatial Framework for Assessing Vegetation Dynamics in Mountainous Areas

Case study: Nyanga National Park, Zimbabwe (2003–2023) · Supervised by Dr. I. Gwitira, Department of Geography, Geospatial Science and Earth Observation, University of Zimbabwe.

Project Brief

An Honours-level geospatial framework combining satellite remote sensing, machine learning and climate modelling to track and forecast vegetation health across Nyanga National Park's Afromontane ecosystems — built from Landsat 5/7/8/9 imagery (2003–2023), validated with Kappa-checked accuracy assessment, and projected forward to 2063 under four CMIP6 SSP pathways.

The Problem

Nyanga's mountain ecosystems had no continuous, spatially complete monitoring record — field surveys can't cover 20 years across a whole park — and no way to separate climate drivers from human pressure, leaving conservation spend undirected and reactive.

Who It Benefits

Park rangers and management get a map-based early-warning system for degrading zones; policymakers get evidence for where to prioritise protection; surrounding communities benefit from a healthier watershed and more resilient tourism asset.

Value It Creates

Directing ranger patrols and restoration budgets to the highest-risk zones — instead of spreading spend evenly — cuts wasted field cost and strengthens funding applications to climate-adaptation bodies (GCF, GEF-type funds) that require exactly this kind of quantified, scenario-based evidence.

Three Objectives

  1. 1

    Map spatiotemporal vegetation trajectories across the park, 2003–2023.

  2. 2

    Attribute the drivers of that change using Random Forest regression.

  3. 3

    Project future vegetation vulnerability under four CMIP6 SSP pathways.

Methods

  • Landsat 5/7/8/9 harmonisation & medoid compositing
  • kNDVI, EVI, NDWI index derivation
  • OLS & Mann-Kendall / Sen's slope trend analysis
  • Getis-Ord Gi* hotspot detection
  • Pearson inter-predictor screening (|r| ≥ 0.70)
  • Random Forest driver attribution, 14 predictors
  • CMIP6 SSP1-2.6 → SSP5-8.5, elevation-dependent warming
  • Monte Carlo uncertainty quantification

Validation

Classification accuracy verified with confusion matrices and Kappa coefficients; SSP projections exported as delta rasters and cross-checked with a ±0.05 residual threshold across three confidence classes.

Beyond the dissertation

Why This Matters to Employers

Every project on this page follows the same loop: find a real gap, build a technically rigorous fix, then translate it into something a decision-maker or funder can act on. That's the skill I bring to a team — not just running the analysis, but knowing what it's for.

Funding & Policy Relevance

The dissertation's output — spatially explicit vulnerability maps under multiple future climate scenarios, with confidence classes attached — is the kind of evidence conservation agencies and climate-adaptation funders look for, rather than a purely academic diagnosis. It identifies where intervention is needed and under what climate trajectory, giving Zimbabwe Parks & Wildlife, EMA, or regional climate-resilience programmes a ready-made evidence base for prioritising conservation spend in Nyanga's most vulnerable zones.

Applied Tool · Live Data

Nyanga Field Weather Dashboard

Live conditions for the dissertation study area, pulled client-side from Open-Meteo — no server, no key, refreshed on load.

Project Brief

A self-contained, live weather dashboard for the exact dissertation study area — built to prove I can ship working software, not just static analysis, and to demonstrate real-time API integration end to end.

The Problem

Static dissertation figures can't show current field conditions, and a broken or placeholder embed undermines trust in an otherwise data-driven portfolio.

Who It Benefits

Anyone planning fieldwork in Nyanga — researchers, rangers, or tour operators — gets an always-current view of conditions with zero setup and zero cost.

Value It Creates

Zero-infrastructure design (no server, no API key, no hosting bill) means this pattern can be embedded free into any park-management or tourism site — proof I can deliver client-ready tools at effectively no ongoing cost.
