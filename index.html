Where is Nyanga’s mountain vegetation changing, and what comes next?

QuestionPark managers needed to know where vegetation is shifting, what is driving it, and which areas are most at risk.
What I didBuilt a Google Earth Engine pipeline on harmonised Landsat 5, 7, 8 and 9 imagery (2003–2023). Measured change with kNDVI, EVI and NDWI, tested trends with Mann-Kendall, found hotspots with Getis-Ord G*, and used Random Forest to rank the drivers.
ChallengeEarly climate projections came out nearly identical across scenarios. I traced it to collinear predictors and poorly scaled sensitivity settings, re-screened the inputs (Pearson |r| ≥ 0.70) and rebuilt the outputs so each pathway gave its own result.
ResultA 20-year view of change, 14 drivers ranked by importance, and vulnerability projections for four climate pathways with Monte Carlo uncertainty. This was my Honours dissertation.
  • Google Earth Engine
  • Landsat
  • Random Forest
  • Mann-Kendall
  • Getis-Ord G*
  • CMIP6

How much green space has the University of Zimbabwe campus lost, and where?

QuestionCampus growth was widely felt but never measured.
What I didBuilt dry-season Sentinel-2 composites for 2018 and 2024 and classified them with k-means on NDVI, NDBI, NDWI and brightness. Then ran transition, fragmentation and loss-hotspot analysis.
ResultFour mapped land-cover classes across two years, with loss hotspots and fragmentation statistics that planners can use.
  • Sentinel-2
  • k-means
  • Change detection
  • Python
